Prop Desk privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026. Applies to the Prop Desk iPhone app and the website at bikrambedi91-svg.github.io/vanguard-desk.

The short version

Prop Desk does not collect, sell or share your data. Your journal stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your trades, notes, screenshots, payouts and settings are saved on your device only: in the app's own storage on iPhone, or in your browser's storage on the website.
  • On iPhone the app also keeps a daily backup file in the app's folder in the Files app, so you can find and move it yourself.
  • The developer never receives this information.

Optional GitHub sync

If you choose to turn on sync, the app sends your journal from your device directly to a private GitHub repository that you own, using an access token you create and paste in. That data is then stored by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement. The token stays on your device. The developer has no access to your repository or your token. You can turn sync off at any time in Setup.

Notifications

Reminders, such as the inactivity warning, are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Exporting and deleting

You can export everything as a backup file or CSV from Setup, and delete an account's data from the Danger zone in Setup. Deleting the app removes its on-device data. Backups you saved elsewhere, and any GitHub repository you connected, stay under your control.

Children

Prop Desk is a tool for adult traders and is not directed to children.

Not affiliated

Prop Desk is an independent journal and calculator. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any proprietary trading firm, broker or exchange. Rule presets are provided for convenience; always confirm the current rules with your firm.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: Prop Desk support.
