Prop Desk support

A trade journal and position-size calculator for futures prop-firm accounts.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/bikrambedi91-svg/vanguard-desk/issues. Please don't include account numbers, tokens or other private details.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your device. On iPhone there is also a daily backup in Files › On My iPhone › Prop Desk › Backups. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my journal to a new phone? Share a backup from Setup, then choose "Restore a backup" on the new phone. Or turn on GitHub sync on both devices.

The rules changed at my firm. Edit them in Setup › Rule values. The app never trades or connects to your broker.

Is this financial advice? No. Prop Desk records your trades and does arithmetic on the limits you set. It doesn't recommend trades.
